FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about how UTME prep works at Himaayah Academy.

How does payment work for UTME prep?

UTME prep is paid monthly.

Are the weekly mock exams compulsory?

Yes. Weekly mocks are compulsory for every UTME student.

Is joining the reading club compulsory?

Yes. Every UTME student is required to join the reading club.

What is the reading club?

The reading club is where students set reading targets and stick with them, so steady reading becomes part of their routine.

Are classes live or recorded?

Classes are held live, and recordings are available if you miss a session or want to review one.
