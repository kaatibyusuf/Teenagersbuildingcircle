Exam Calendars

Registration and sitting windows shift a little each year, set by the exam bodies themselves — not by us. Here's the general rhythm, and how to get the exact confirmed dates.

Exact dates change year to year. The windows below are the typical pattern, not confirmed 2026 dates. Email us at himaayahschools@gmail.com for the current confirmed calendar.
UTME (JAMB)

Registration, then a CBT sitting a few months later

Registration typically opens early in the year, with the exam itself sitting a few months after. Our UTME track runs year-round so you're not starting prep once the registration window opens.

WAEC / NECO

Registration in the first quarter, exams mid-year

WAEC and NECO registration for SS3 students usually opens early in the year, with the exams themselves sitting mid-year. School-based candidates register through their school; private candidates register directly.

A-Levels (IJMB/JUPEB)

Session-based, not a single fixed date

IJMB and JUPEB run on an academic-session calendar rather than a single exam day — registration and program start dates vary by affiliated institution.

Post-UTME

Set by each institution, after UTME results are out

Every institution sets its own Post-UTME/screening date once JAMB releases results — there's no single national date. We follow announcements per institution as they come out.

Don't wait for the deadline to prepare.

By the time registration opens, our students are already months into structured prep. Start now, whichever exam is ahead of you.
