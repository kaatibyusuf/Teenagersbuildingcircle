Questions people ask first.

Anything else, ask us directly and we will get back to you.

Ask us
Who can join?

Anyone who wants to learn a skill properly. This track starts from the basics, so a complete beginner can begin on day one.

What do I need to take part?

A laptop or computer and a steady internet connection.

How are classes held?

Classes are live online. There is no fixed timetable. After you enrol, your teacher agrees the days and hour with you, around school, work and family.

How long is the track?

Eighteen months, in three phases of six months: Foundations, Practice and Ship.

Can I change my track after I pay?

Plans cannot be changed after the registration fee is paid. A change means a fresh registration, so choose carefully. The track matcher on the Skills Schools page can help you decide.

Where do I ask a question?

Message us on WhatsApp on +234 704 722 6487, or use the contact page.
