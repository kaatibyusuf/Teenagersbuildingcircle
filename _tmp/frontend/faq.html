Head to Enroll and work through three short steps: student information, then your programme and plan, then payment. It takes about two minutes. At the end, pay by card on a secure payment page and your place is confirmed straight away, or pay by bank transfer and send your receipt to us on WhatsApp using a message that is already written for you.

Card or bank transfer. At the last step of enrollment, choose Card to pay on a secure Bachs payment page, or Bank transfer to pay into our account. Card payments are confirmed straight away, and your student login is emailed to you. After a transfer, the page opens WhatsApp with your details and plan already filled in. Attach your receipt and send it, and we confirm your place, usually within a few hours.
