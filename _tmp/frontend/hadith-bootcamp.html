Ḥadīth 1: Actions are by intentions

عَنْ أَمِيرِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أَبِي حَفْصٍ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ يَقُولُ: إنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى، فَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ إلَى اللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ فَهِجْرَتُهُ إلَى اللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ، وَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ لِدُنْيَا يُصِيبُهَا أَوْ امْرَأَةٍ يَنْكِحُهَا فَهِجْرَتُهُ إلَى مَا هَاجَرَ إلَيْهِ

I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say: “Actions are only by intentions, and every person will have only what he intended. So whoever emigrated for Allah and His Messenger, his emigration is for Allah and His Messenger; and whoever emigrated for some worldly thing he wants to gain, or a woman he wants to marry, his emigration is for what he emigrated for.”

Narrated by ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb. Al-Bukhārī (1) and Muslim (1907).

Questions

Do I need to know Arabic?

No. Every ḥadīth comes with the Arabic text and an English translation, and the lessons are explained in English. If you do read Arabic, you will get even more from it.

How long does it take?

It is self-paced. At one ḥadīth a day you will finish in about six weeks; some students take longer, some go faster. Your progress is saved, so you can stop and continue any time.

Can I study on my phone?

Yes. The portal works on any phone, tablet or computer with an internet connection. The audio plays in the page.

How do I get the certificate?

Pass the short quiz after each of the 42 ḥadīth (4 out of 5 correct; you can retry). When all 42 are passed, download your certificate from the portal. Each certificate has a number anyone can check on this page.

I paid but did not get my login.

Your Bootcamp ID and access code are emailed within a few minutes of payment. Check your spam folder. If it has not arrived after 30 minutes, message the Madrasah line on WhatsApp with your payment email.

I am a Himaayah Madrasah student. Do I pay?

No. Log in to the bootcamp with the Student ID and access code you already use for your dashboard.

Check a certificate

Every Ḥadīth Bootcamp certificate has a number such as HBC-7K2M9Q. Enter it to confirm it is genuine.
