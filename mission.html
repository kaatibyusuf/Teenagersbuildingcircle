Our mission and vision.

What Himaayah Schools is here to do, and the future we are working toward.

Our vision
A generation grounded in deen, strong in knowledge, and ready to serve with purpose.

We want every student who comes through Himaayah to leave better prepared for this life and the next.

Our mission: four commitments.

Everything we teach, across every programme, comes back to these four.

  1. 1

    Ground students in deen

    Teach the faith and its practice in a way students can carry beyond the classroom.

    Explore the Madrasah
  2. 2

    Build strong academic foundations

    Give students the structure, teaching and practice they need to succeed in the exams that open doors.

    Explore the Academy
  3. 3

    Develop practical skills

    Equip students with abilities they can actually use, from public speaking to graphic design and data analysis.

    Explore Skills School
  4. 4

    Shape character and purpose

    Nurture discipline, consistency and good character, so students grow into people who use what they know well.

    Explore the Teenagers Building Circle
