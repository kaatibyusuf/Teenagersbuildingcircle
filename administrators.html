Founder

Kaatib Yusuf

Writer, educator, entrepreneur, and systems builder

  • FounderOne Growth
  • AuthorFacing The Dreams You Let Die

Kaatib Yusuf is a Nigerian writer, educator, entrepreneur, and systems builder whose work sits at the intersection of writing, education, personal development, faith, and human formation.

He is the founder of One Growth, a personal development organisation that has reached thousands of young people through mentorship, writing, and academic initiatives.

His book Facing The Dreams You Let Die has reached more than 16,000 readers across four continents. Much of his work asks how knowledge can be matched with the discipline, courage, and moral framework needed to use it well, and he is committed to building institutions that outlive their founders.
