Class Levels

Five levels, one progression.

Tamheediyyah through Raabi'ah Ibtidaaiyyah — each level building on the last, across the same core subjects.

Level I

Tamheediyyah

Foundation level — first exposure to Arabic letters, basic recitation, and the earliest Islamic concepts.

Hadeeth Tawheed Arabiyyah Tajweed Seerah + more
Level II

Ula Ibtidaaiyyah

Primary 1 — building reading fluency and introducing grammar fundamentals alongside continued memorisation.

Hadeeth Tawheed Arabiyyah Sarf Nahw Tajweed Seerah + more
Level III

Thaniyah Ibtidaaiyyah

Primary 2 — deeper grammar (sarf and nahw) and a wider hadeeth and seerah foundation.

Hadeeth Tawheed Arabiyyah Sarf Nahw Tajweed Seerah + more
Level IV

Thaalithah Ibtidaaiyyah

Primary 3 — more independent reading and writing in Arabic, with grammar applied rather than just studied.

Hadeeth Tawheed Arabiyyah Sarf Nahw Tajweed Seerah + more
Level V

Raabi'ah Ibtidaaiyyah

Primary 4 — the most advanced level, consolidating everything before a student moves on.

Hadeeth Tawheed Arabiyyah Sarf Nahw Tajweed Seerah + more
