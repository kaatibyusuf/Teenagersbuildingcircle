How do I enroll?

Choose a plan and select Enroll now. Complete the three enrolment steps, pay by bank transfer, then send your receipt to us on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

Pay by bank transfer and send the receipt on WhatsApp. Card payment is coming soon. Payment is made once a month, in a single payment.

Where are classes held?

Classes are held on Google Meet and Telegram.

When will my teacher be assigned?

Immediately after payment, a Hifdh student is assigned to a teacher.

How does the madrasah year work?

We run two semesters in a year. Each semester is four months.

Who can join?

From school age to adults, depending on the plan. Each level lists what a student should already know.

Can I change my plan?

Once your payment is confirmed, the plan can't be changed, so choose the plan that fits before you pay. If you are unsure, message us on WhatsApp first.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds can't be made after a week of classes.

How do I check my progress, payments and assignments?

Sign in to the student dashboard with your Student ID and access code. We email these to you once your payment is confirmed.
