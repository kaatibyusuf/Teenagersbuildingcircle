Ask us
Who can join?

Anyone who wants to learn a skill properly. Every track starts from the basics, so a complete beginner can begin on day one.

What do I need to take part?

A laptop or computer and a steady internet connection. Video Creation can begin with a phone.

How are classes held?

Classes are live online. There is no fixed timetable. After you enrol, your teacher agrees the days and hour with you, around school, work and family.

How long is each track?

Eighteen months, in three phases of six months: Foundations, Practice and Ship.

Can I change my track after I pay?

Plans cannot be changed after the registration fee is paid. A change means a fresh registration, so choose carefully. The track matcher above can help you decide.

Where do I ask a question?

Message us on WhatsApp on +234 704 722 6487, or use the contact page.
