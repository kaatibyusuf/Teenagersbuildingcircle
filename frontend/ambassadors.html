The role

What an Ambassador does.

An Ambassador is a trusted face of Himaayah. These are the duties, and the lines you must never cross.

Your duties

  • Represent Himaayah honestly, using only what the school publishes.
  • Introduce the programmes to families, students and communities, online and in person.
  • Share your own link or code, and point people to the placement test and free guides.
  • Send enquiries to the right WhatsApp line: Academy, Madrasah or General.
  • Join a monthly check-in and pass on what families tell you.
  • Help at events such as open days, campus talks and Ramadan drives.

What you must never do

  • Promise results, discounts or admission, or quote fees that are not on our website.
  • Collect money for the school. Payments go only to the school account or the card checkout.
  • Pressure, mislead or spam people, or message anyone who has not agreed to hear from you.
  • Say you are staff, or use our name and crest in ways our brand rules do not allow.
  • Share any student’s private details.
  • Refer yourself or your own household for a reward.

Talk to us
Is it free to join?

Yes. Joining costs nothing, and you are never asked to pay or to buy anything.

When do I get paid?

When someone enrols through your link or code and their payment is confirmed, 10% of their first fee goes to the bank account you gave us. Your dashboard shows each referral, and we email you when a payment is sent.

Can I apply if I am under 18?

Yes, from age 16, with a parent or guardian’s agreement. We contact them as part of the review, and you may be paid into their account.

What is the difference between this and Refer and Earn?

Refer and Earn is open to everyone with no review. Ambassadors are chosen, held to a code of conduct and recognised for it. The 10% reward is the same.

How long does approval take?

We aim to reply within a week. A decision is sent to the email you gave us.
