Submit to the Journal

Open all year

Everyone is welcome to send Arabic writing: students, alumni, parents and guests. We read new work as it arrives. Read the guidelines below, then use the form.

Go to the form Read the journal

At a glance

Who can send
Anyone: students, alumni, parents and guests.
Language
Arabic. A translation may include the original, but the Arabic must be there.
What we accept
Poems, short stories, essays, reflections, translations and research.
Length
Paste up to 20,000 characters. For longer work, attach a PDF of up to 7 MB.
How many
Up to three submissions a day from one email address.
Cost
Free. There is no fee to send work.
Review
Our staff read every piece before it is published. There is no fixed deadline.
Privacy
Your email address is never shown on the site.

What we accept

Poem

Verse in any form, classical or free. Keep each line of verse on its own line.

Short story

Fiction with a beginning, a middle and an end, told in clear Arabic.

Essay

An argued piece on a subject that matters to you, with its sources credited.

Reflection

A short, personal piece of thought or feeling.

Translation

A piece you have translated into or from Arabic. Name the original author and work, and credit the source.

Research paper

A study with a clear question, evidence and references. Attach a PDF for long papers.

How it works

  1. Send it in. Fill in the form below and paste your full text. A PDF is optional.
  2. We read it. Staff read the piece for language, accuracy and our standards.
  3. We may write to you. If we suggest changes, we will email the address you gave.
  4. It is published. Approved work appears in the journal under your name.

Guidelines

Your own work

Send only work you wrote or translated yourself. Anything quoted must be credited to its source.

Sound Arabic

We may suggest corrections to spelling, grammar and wording before publishing.

In line with our standards

Writing on religion follows the Sunni scholarly tradition. Quote Qurʼānic verses and ḥadīth accurately and name the source.

Respectful

No insults, no mockery of religion, and nothing that targets a person or a group.

Published elsewhere?

Say so in the line about you, with where and when it appeared.

What we look for

Writing that is clear, honest and carefully edited. Short pieces are welcome.

Before you send

  • It is in Arabic, and every line of verse is on its own line.
  • I have read it aloud and corrected the mistakes I found.
  • Anything I quoted is credited.
  • I used an email address I check, so you can reach me.
  • If it is long, I have attached a PDF.
