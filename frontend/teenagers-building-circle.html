A typical week

What a week actually looks like.

Every student starts with a diagnostic assessment, so the cohort isn't a guess. It's built around where each student actually is. From there, it's live weekly sessions with real facilitators, backed by a dedicated Slack workspace that keeps the conversation going the rest of the week.

  • A diagnostic assessment before the cohort begins
  • Live weekly sessions across all four pillars
  • Session times are set with your facilitator when the cohort forms
  • A dedicated Slack workspace for the cohort
  • Progress check-ins built around each pillar
