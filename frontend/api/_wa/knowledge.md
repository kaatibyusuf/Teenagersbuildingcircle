TO INTENDING PARENTS AND STUDENTS
A welcome from our Founder
Assalāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāh.
Every parent who writes to us is asking the same quiet question: will my child grow into someone
the world can trust? Every student who enrols is asking a quieter one: can I become more than I am
today? This prospectus is our answer to both, and the answer is yes, when the work is done
properly.
Himaayah was founded on a belief I hold firmly. Knowledge is a trust. Allah places it in a person’s
hands together with opportunity and responsibility, and a person who receives all three without
discipline, courage and a moral compass will drop what they were given. This school exists to build
the person who can carry it.
That is why we refuse to be a place that only helps a student finish. Any institution can help a child
pass an exam, memorise a page or collect a certificate. We want the surah still on the tongue in ten
years, the study habit still alive in the first job, and the character still standing when nobody is
watching. What lasts is what we build, and we build it with structure: a dedicated teacher for every
class, honest testing, and a monthly report that tells a family the truth.
Our name, Himāyah, means protection, and we take it as a promise. We protect a student’s faith
from the noise around it, their focus from the distractions that cut it into pieces, and their future
from the idea that it is too late or too far away. A protected student is free to grow, and a student
who grows becomes someone others can lean on.
Look at who walks through our doors: a child learning the letters of the Qur’an, a candidate
preparing for the paper that decides a course, a teenager learning to run a day well, a beginner
building a first real skill. Every one of them is a person being made, one lesson at a time. That is the
work, and it is why I do it.
Read these pages slowly. Take a placement test. Ask us the hard questions. Then join us, and let us
help you or your child carry knowledge well, in shā’ Allāh. We would be honoured to teach you.
Kaatib Yusuf
Founder, Himaayah Schools

 01  Who we are
Himaayah Schools is a school for students who want a real relationship with the
Qur’an, strong academics, and the discipline to carry both through life.
 Our story
Himaayah is two years old, and every year we have deepened what we teach. Today we run four
programmes under one roof, for students who want the Qur’an and Islamic knowledge, top exam
results, practical skills, or all three.
Behind every class is the same promise: taught properly, and paced to the student in front of us. A
student is never rushed past what they have not yet learned, and never held back from what they
are ready for.

 What the Full Madrasah teaches
Hifdh and tajweed are taught together with Tawḥīd (creed), Fiqh (worship and its rulings),
Arabiyyah (the Arabic language), Ṣarf (morphology), Naḥw (grammar), Seerah, Ḥadīth and
Maḥfūẓāt (memorised texts). The Hifdh Only plans focus entirely on memorisation, tajweed and
murājaʿah.
 Levels and admission scores
Every student starts at the right level. Entry levels need only a short placement conversation; higher
levels have a minimum admission score and set knowledge, checked by a timed online placement
test in Arabic at himaayahschools.com/placement.html.
