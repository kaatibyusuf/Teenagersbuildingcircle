Submit your work

Send us your writing.

The Himaayah Literary Journal is open to everyone: students, alumni, parents and guests. We welcome Arabic poems, short stories, essays, reflections, translations and research. Our team reads every piece before it is published, and your email is never shown.

  1. You send it in. Paste your full text into the form. A PDF is optional.
  2. We review it. Our staff read it and may contact you with notes.
  3. It is published. Approved work appears here under your name for everyone to read.
