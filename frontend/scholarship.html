Also required

Added by Himaayah Schools. Please read them before you apply.

Applications open
  1. What counts as A's and B's. Every subject on the result you upload must be A1, B2 or B3 (WAEC or NECO), with at least five subjects including English Language and Mathematics. A C4, C5, C6, D7, E8 or F9 in any subject means you are not eligible.
  2. Result already released. You must already hold your WAEC or NECO result. We cannot accept applications from students still waiting for results.
  3. Sitting the 2027 UTME. You must be writing the 2027 UTME and must not have paid for the 2027 UTME form already.
  4. Your result must be genuine. We check results with the examination body. A false or altered result removes you from the scholarship for good.
  5. About the exam. It is scored out of 400, like the UTME. "Above 290" means 291 or more. You sit it once, at the time and in the way we announce. If you miss it, you are out of the selection.
  6. How the 20 are chosen. The 20 highest scorers who meet every requirement. If scores tie, the student with more A1s is placed first, then the one who applied earlier. If fewer than 20 students qualify, fewer than 20 are awarded.
  7. What is paid. Himaayah Schools pays the UTME registration form fee. We do not cover CBT-centre charges, transport, textbooks or post-UTME fees. We pay the fee ourselves and do not hand cash to students.
  8. After you win. You must register for the 2027 UTME when we tell you to, and send us your registration slip. If you do not, your place passes to the next student in line.
  9. Who can apply. Anyone writing the 2027 UTME, from any school. One application per person. Applications close on 15 December 2026. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must be named on the form and agree to it.
  10. Your information. We use your details and result only for this scholarship. The committee's decision is final.
