How referrals work

  • You earn 10% of the fee the new student pays when they enrol, once their payment is confirmed.
  • It applies to every programme: Madrasah, Academy, Teenagers Building Circle and Skills School.
  • The new student must enrol with your link or type your referral code on the enrolment form.
  • Each enrolment counts once, for one referrer. Referring yourself or your own child does not count.
  • If an enrolment is refunded, the referral earning is cancelled.
  • The school sends earnings by bank transfer to the account you give us, and emails you when it is sent. Keep your bank details up to date on this page.
