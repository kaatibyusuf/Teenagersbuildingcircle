Admission, step by step.

Whichever programme you choose, getting in follows the same clear path: choose, get placed, enroll, pay, and confirm. Here is everything you need before you begin.

Start enrollment Ask on WhatsApp Download prospectus
The steps

Five steps from interest to first class.

It takes about two minutes online. Most of the waiting is for us to confirm your payment, which is usually within a few hours.

Choose your programme

Madrasah, Academy, Skills School or the Teenagers Building Circle. Not sure which exam track? Take a free CBT mock test to see where you stand.

Get placed at the right level

Madrasah students take an online placement test for the level they are applying to, marked instantly with a 70% pass mark; Rawdah and Tamheedi need only a short conversation. Circle students begin with a diagnostic assessment. This is how each class fits the student, not the other way round.

Fill in the enrollment form

Three short steps on our Enroll page: student information, programme and plan, then payment.

Pay by bank transfer

Transfer the plan's fee to our Wema Bank account. The exact account and amount are shown on the last step of the form.

Send your receipt on WhatsApp

The form opens WhatsApp with a message already written for your plan. Attach your receipt and send, and we confirm your place.

Madrasah levels and minimum scores.

These levels are not taking new students right now. Private (one-on-one) classes for Full Madrasah and Hifdh Only are still open. Join the waitlist
LevelMinimum scoreWhat the student must already know
RawdahOpen entryNothing. For those who can’t read and write at all.
TamheediOpen entryFor those who are just starting madrasah. Some Arabic letters, or ready to learn them quickly.
Awwal Ibtidā’iyyah75%Recognise and pronounce the Arabic letters, read simple words, write letters and simple words, and basic knowledge of Ṣalāh, Ṭahārah and manners.
Thāniyah Ibtidā’iyyah80%10 Hadeeth of an-Nawawī, familiarity with short texts and adhkār, reading Arabic letters and words, and a basic understanding of manners, worship and creed.
Thālithah Ibtidā’iyyah85%15 Hadeeth of an-Nawawī, the basic principles of al-Uṣūl ath-Thalāthah, the introductory portion of Durūs al-Lughah al-ʿArabiyyah, and reasonable reading fluency.
Rābiʿah Ibtidā’iyyah90%20 Hadeeth of an-Nawawī, al-Uṣūl ath-Thalāthah memorised, the first series of Durūs completed, and fluent reading with correct basic writing.
Awwal Iʿdādiyyah90%The Forty Aḥādīth of an-Nawawī, al-Uṣūl ath-Thalāthah, Lāmiyyah of Ibn Taymiyyah, the prescribed Dīwān of Imām ash-Shāfiʿī, both series of Durūs, and strong Arabic reading and writing. These are non-negotiable.
