Placement tests

Find your level. Know where you stand.

Free online tests, marked the moment you finish. Madrasah students sit a test in Arabic for the level they are applying to. UTME candidates sit a timed mock in English and three subjects from their department. Each Madrasah level keeps its own pass mark, from 75% to 90%; the UTME cut-off is 70%.

Before you start
  • The timer starts as soon as you press Start and keeps running if you leave the page. When it ends, your answers are sent automatically.
  • Take the test on your own, without books, notes, phones or help. It is only useful if it is honest.
  • You can move between questions and change answers until you submit. A question left blank scores zero.
  • You can take the same test once every 24 hours.
