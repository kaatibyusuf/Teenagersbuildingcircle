Sponsor a student

Help a child keep learning.

Some families want their children to memorise the Qur'an, prepare for exams or learn a skill, but cannot afford the fees. You can pay for a student's classes, or give to the Sponsorship Fund and let the school choose who needs it most.

The fund pays fees for students whose families cannot afford them. Staff choose who it helps, and we email you when your gift is used.

Your details

You pay on the secure Bachs payment page by card or bank transfer. Prefer a direct transfer? Send it to Himaayah Ltd, Wema Bank, 0127584920, with the note "Sponsor", and message us on WhatsApp.

Where your gift goes

  • Straight to fees. Your gift pays the student's school fees. It is not spent on anything else.
  • Families are checked. Staff speak to every family who applies before a student is listed.
  • You hear back. Sponsors of a listed student get a short progress update each month. Fund donors are told when their gift is used.
  • Zakat? This page is for sadaqah. If you want to give zakat, message us first so we can make sure it reaches families who are eligible.
