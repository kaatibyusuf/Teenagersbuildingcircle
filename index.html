Questions people ask first.

Anything else, ask us directly and we will get back to you.

How do I enroll?

Head to Enroll and work through three short steps: student information, your programme and plan, then payment. It takes about two minutes.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer. After you pay, the enrollment page opens WhatsApp with your details already filled in. Attach your receipt and send it. Card payment is coming soon.

What ages do you accept?

Teenagers Building Circle is for ages 13 to 19. Madrasah, Academy and Skills School are open more broadly, from school-age students to adult learners, depending on the plan.

Can I change my plan after enrolling?

Yes. Email us at himaayahschools@gmail.com with your name and current plan and we will help you switch.
